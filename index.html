    How it fixes the real problem

    Companies lose money when every request goes to the strongest, most expensive model by default. One enterprise reportedly spent $500 million in a single month with no controls in place.

    Read

    The prompt is inspected before it leaves the page: length, code, reasoning words, and simple lookup patterns.

    Score

    It gets a 0 to 100 importance score and a level: simple, moderate, or complex.

    Route

    The cheapest model you have access to at that level is chosen. You can always override it.

    Costs use illustrative list prices (US$ per million tokens) at ₹90 per US$ and rough token estimates. Your real bill will differ.
